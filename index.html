A page about the BMW E46 M3

The E46 M3 is the best selling M3 of all time, selling over 86,000 units between 2000-2006.

The BMW S54B32 engine won eight International Engine of the Year awards between 2001 and 2006 .

Stats and facts

  • Powered by a naturally aspirated 3.2-liter (S54B32) straight-six engine that revs up to 8,000 RPM.
  • Max. output: 343 - 360 hp.
  • The E46 M3 is often regarded as the best M3 ever made
  • It is my goal to own a E46 M3 by the end of 2028.
